Transforming Diagnostic Integration with SAP

With 35+ years of SAP consulting expertise, MyTekX (formerly Nexlink) led the integration of two diagnostic companies into SAP for Siemens Healthineers — solving complex IFRS15 compliance and bundled contract challenges that SAP itself could not address natively.

Challenges Faced

  • Complex bundled contracts with multi-element arrangements (MEA)
  • Full regulatory compliance with IFRS 15
  • Precise transfer of legacy sales and financial data
  • SAP lacked a native solution — previous attempts had failed

MyTekX Solution

  • Custom Contract & Lease Management (CLM) with Revenue Recognition (RAR)
  • Addressed MEA complexities and full IFRS compliance
  • Accurate data migration with full audit trail
  • 2016: Custom RAR for Invivo — foreshadowed SAP's own RAR offering
